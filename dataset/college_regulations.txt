COLLEGE REGULATIONS

Attendance

Students must maintain at least 75% attendance.

Students with attendance below 75% require medical approval.

Students below 60% attendance cannot appear in semester examinations.

-------------------------------------

Examination Rules

Students must carry a valid college ID card.

Electronic devices including mobile phones are prohibited.

Late entry beyond 30 minutes is not allowed.

-------------------------------------

Grading System

90–100 : A+

80–89 : A

70–79 : B+

60–69 : B

50–59 : C

Below 50 : Fail

-------------------------------------

Library Rules

Maximum 3 books can be borrowed.

Issue period is 14 days.

Fine is Rs.10 per day for overdue books.

-------------------------------------

Hostel Rules

Visitors are allowed from 5 PM to 7 PM.

No loud music after 10 PM.

-------------------------------------

Scholarship

Students scoring above 85% are eligible for merit scholarships.
